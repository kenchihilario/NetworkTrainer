Inside the box (angas noh, [praise nyo aq dali]):

  1. In jacks (3). Laptop cables plug in here.
  2. Relays. 3 modules, 8 relays each. Every wire goes through one. Switch it and that wire is cut.
  3. Out jacks (3). Cables to the switch.
  4. ESP32. Runs the Wi-Fi and web page and controls the relays. Network data never goes through it.
  5. I/O expanders (2). Let two ESP32 pins run all 24 relays and 6 LEDs.
  6. OLED. Shows which laptop is cut and the fault.
  7. LEDs (3 red, 3 green). Show which cable is cut.
  8. Power (5 V, 3 A). Runs the ESP32 and relays.

Blue or red lines cable (red when cut). Orange control wires. Black power. Orange relays are on, so that cable is open.
